# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is a new admin page that gives you a quick overview of your donations and campaign performance at a glance.

---

## Overview

When the Plus add-on is active, the Dashboard replaces the default Kudos Donations landing page in the WordPress admin. It aggregates data from all your campaigns and transactions into a set of visual charts and summary cards.

## Score Cards

At the top of the Dashboard you will find a row of score cards showing key totals at a glance, such as the total amount donated, number of transactions, and number of active donors.

## Charts

### Transactions over time

A line chart showing the number and value of donations over time, grouped by month. Useful for spotting trends in donation activity.

### Donations by campaign

A bar chart breaking down the total donations received per campaign so you can see which campaigns are performing best.

### Donors by country

A pie chart showing the geographic spread of your donors based on the country recorded at the time of donation.

## Customising the layout

You can adjust the number of columns used to display the charts and hide any charts you do not need. These preferences are saved automatically and persist between sessions.
