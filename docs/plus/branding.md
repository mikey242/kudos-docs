# Branding

The **Branding** settings allow you to replace the default Kudos Donations branding with your own logo and optionally hide Kudos branding entirely from donor-facing elements.

---

## Overview

Branding options are found in the **Customise** tab of the Kudos Donations settings page. The settings apply globally across all campaigns.

## Custom Logo

You can upload or select an image from your WordPress Media Library to use as your organisation's logo. Once set, this logo will be used in:

- Donation receipt emails sent to donors
- PDF receipts

### Selecting a logo

1. Go to **Kudos Donations → Settings → Customise**.
2. Under **Custom logo**, click **Select image**.
3. Choose an existing image from your Media Library or upload a new one.
4. Save your settings.

## Hide Kudos Branding

Enabling **Hide Kudos branding** removes the Kudos Donations branding from:

- Donate buttons
- Donation forms

This gives your donation experience a cleaner, white-label appearance.

### Enabling

1. Go to **Kudos Donations → Settings → Customise**.
2. Under **Kudos Branding**, toggle **Hide Kudos branding** on.
3. Save your settings.
