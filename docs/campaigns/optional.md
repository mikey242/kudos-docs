# Optional fields

Here you can change the availability of various optional fields on the donation form.

---

## Donor details

### Name

Controls whether the name field is shown on the donation form.

#### Required

When enabled, donors must enter their name before submitting.

### Email

Controls whether the email address field is shown on the donation form.

#### Required

When enabled, donors must enter their email address before submitting.

### Address

When enabled this will show the address section.

#### Required

Make the address section a required field.

### Message

Enables the message section allowing donors to leave a custom message.

## Policy links

Before submitting the donation form you can require your donors to agree to your Terms and conditions and/or privacy policy. Leave these blank to disable.
